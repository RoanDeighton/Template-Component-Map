A white card overlapping the page header: a staff member's photo and quote ("Hoi, ik ben Merel!"), a short checklist of highlights, and a "Kennismaken" contact box (address, phone, email, location manager) alongside it. The main distinguishing block of an individual location's own page, as opposed to a city-level overview page like [kinderopvang-apeldoorn](../pages/kinderopvang-apeldoorn.md).

Two things vary across the 4 locations sampled, independently of each other — see Variants:

- **Action buttons** ("Schrijf je kind in", "Rondleiding?") under the address line: present on 3 of 4, absent on `kinderopvang-arnhem-bso-callunastraat-54`. No obvious rule found for why that one doesn't have them — worth checking against a larger sample.
- **Type-switcher tab bar**: only on `kinderopvang-vught-bso-koninginnelaan-1a`, the one sampled address that offers more than one care type (kinderdagverblijf, bso, peuteropvang) in the same building.
