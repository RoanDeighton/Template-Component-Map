A full-height photo on one side and a white card overlapping it on the other, holding a large pull-quote and a name/role attribution below. Distinct from [Testimonial Quote](testimonial-quote.md), which uses a circular-cropped headshot and runs 1-3 quotes as equal-sized cards rather than a single quote paired with a full photo.
