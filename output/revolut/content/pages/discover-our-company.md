## Content pattern

This page stitches together two different content types. The top third is brand storytelling: a mission statement, customer stat callouts, and a milestone timeline. The rest of the page is a formal governance registry, a leadership photo grid followed by nine individual board member bios, each with a name, title, and short career summary. It closes with the same award proof points used on other pages before the shared plan comparison and footer.
