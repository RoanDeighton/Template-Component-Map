## Content pattern

The page front-loads the transactional tool (the converter and fee table) for visitors who already want to compare, then spends the rest of its length on SEO-oriented education and trust building: how exchange rates work, security, app breadth, then link farms by currency and by provider aimed at long-tail search terms. The FAQ and closing CTA wrap it up before the shared footer.
