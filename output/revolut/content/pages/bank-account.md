## Content pattern

This page repeats the promo-block-plus-highlight-row rhythm several times over, money monitoring, savings, salary switching, security, before ending on a formal regulatory summary box rather than a plain legal disclaimer. That summary box is heavier and more structured than the legal blocks on the other account pages, closer to a terms sheet than marketing copy.
