## Content pattern

Like the Plus page, this template opens with a run of individual benefit panels (subscriptions, savings rate, card, rewards, protection, support), then compresses everything into a tabbed summary before the comparison prompt. The single "Fine Print Block" at the end replaces the multiple stacked disclaimer sections used on the Plus page, consolidating the legal text into one block instead.
