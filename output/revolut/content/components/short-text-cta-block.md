# Short Text/CTA Block

**Used on:** Currency Converter, Money Transfer Country Page, eSIM Plan Page (18 occurrences — the single most-reused component in this sample)

![Short text/CTA block](../component-crops/short-text-cta-block.png)

A compact block: heading, one short paragraph, one image, one link/button — small enough that it reads as a single beat of content rather than a full section. Used flexibly for two different jobs: a **short FAQ answer** ("Hoeveel kost het om VAE-dirhams naar euro's om te zetten?" / "Hoe lang duurt mijn overschrijving...") and a **short promotional CTA** ("Verzend geld zonder verborgen overschrijvingskosten," "Beveiliging zit ingebouwd in al onze producten," "Wissel VAE-dirhams naar euro's met Revolut"). Structurally identical in both roles — same size, same shape, just different intent behind the copy.

## Occurrences (sample)

- Currency Converter: cost question, closing "convert now" CTA
- Money Transfer: cost question, duration question, no-hidden-fees CTA, security CTA, "send easily" CTA
- eSIM Plan: closing "Zoveel meer dan eSIM" CTA

## Variants observed

Two content roles (FAQ answer vs. promotional CTA) sharing one visual template — worth keeping in mind if this were mapped to a CMS content type, since the two roles likely want different editorial fields even though the layout is identical.
