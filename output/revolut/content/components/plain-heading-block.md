# Plain Heading Block

**Used on:** Money Transfer Country Page, eSIM Plan Page, Card Product: Virtual Card (7 occurrences)

![Plain heading block](../component-crops/plain-heading-block.png)

The simplest content block in the sample: heading + short paragraph, no image, no link, no form — small footprint (1–15 descendant elements). Used as a light section intro or transitional block ("Hoe maak je je eerste overschrijving naar Albanië?," "Data-abonnementen voor je reisplannen," "Soorten virtuele kaarten," "Krijg een lokaal eSIM-plan in Afrika") rather than a content-heavy block in its own right — usually followed by a more substantial component (a list, grid, or selector) that carries the actual content.
